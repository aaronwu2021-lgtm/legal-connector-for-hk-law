# Court of Appeal outcome — extract

Appeal from Deputy High Court Judge Alexander Stock SC (three Ord 14A questions). Dismissed; trial judge affirmed on all questions.

- **Question 1 (Milmo).** The 1983 sub-lease, being for a term exceeding the residue of Madam Tang's Government lease, took effect by virtue of Milmo v Carreras [1946] KB 306 as an assignment of that lease to Mr Cheong Lam To.
- **Question 2 (extension).** The New Territories Leases (Extension) Ordinance (Cap 150) extended the Government lease. Not in issue on the appeal as framed here.
- **Question 3 (s.3(2)).** Mr Cheong Lam To's claims are avoided, as against the property company and the plot purchasers (bona fide purchasers for value), by LRO s.3(2): the unregistered 1983 instrument is null and void as against them. The nemo dat objection was rejected.

The appellant's case throughout: Milmo deemed Madam Tang to have assigned everything in 1983, so she had nothing to give by the 1986 Deed of Gift, so Mr To Shui Ki had nothing to sell, so the plot purchasers took nothing.
