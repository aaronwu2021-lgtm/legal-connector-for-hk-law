Court: Hong Kong Court of Final Appeal.

Source type: merits judgment; FACV Nos. 3 and 5 of 2019 were heard together.

[65]-[68]: an action on an award relies on the implied promise to honour it; breach is a "fresh cause of action separate and independent" from breach of the underlying contract.
[69]-[70]: entry of the statutory judgment was not an informed irrevocable election. The inconsistent remedies "could not stand together", and an informed claimant was "required to make an election".
[122]: for an unperformed non-monetary award, the enforcing court may choose an apt remedy from the "full range of remedies available in an ordinary common law action".

[141]: after the statutory judgment, the claimant began the common law action and "sought damages as an alternative remedy" before making its election.
[141]-[143]: the Court preserved the qualification that a remedy may be refused if the limited scope of an award does not warrant it.
[146]: an existing statutory judgment did not itself prevent the alternative claim. Once the claimant elected damages, the Court of Appeal "set aside the statutory judgment" and entered judgment for damages.
[149]: a claimant may seek inconsistent remedies in the alternative but must elect before judgment; they cannot both stand as final relief.
[148]-[150]: after election, the damages relief "falls within the enforcement jurisdiction"; the extant award was no obstacle on these facts.
