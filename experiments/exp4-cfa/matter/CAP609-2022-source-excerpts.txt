INSTRUMENT: Arbitration Ordinance Cap. 609 ss. 34, 81
Publisher: Hong Kong Department of Justice.
Source type: current-legislation XML.

Section 34 gives effect to UNCITRAL Model Law Article 16. Article 16(3) states that the tribunal may decide the relevant plea "either as a preliminary question or in an award on the merits". A party may request a court decision within 30 days if the tribunal rules as a preliminary question that it has jurisdiction.

Section 81 gives effect to UNCITRAL Model Law Article 34. Article 34(1) states: "Recourse to a court against an arbitral award may be made only by an application for setting aside" in accordance with Article 34(2) and (3). Article 34(2)(a)(iii) applies where "the award deals with a dispute not contemplated by or not falling within the terms of the submission to arbitration", or contains decisions outside the scope of that submission, subject to severability.

The external reader must check sections 34 and 81 in the official version, including section 13(5), the complete incorporated Model Law text, and any version relevant to the task cutoff.
