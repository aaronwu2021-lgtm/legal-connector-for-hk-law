# Client conference note — Pearl Delta Development Ltd

## Facts (agreed, neutral)

1. Pearl obtained a CIETAC arbitral award made in Beijing (27 October 2006)
   ordering two counterparties (D1-D2) to perform a development agreement;
   Pearl later sought to enforce that award in Hong Kong.
2. After the arbitration started, without disclosure to Pearl or the
   tribunal, D1-D2 transferred their shares in the vehicle holding the
   development, so the award became practically unperformable. The tribunal
   later dismissed D1-D2's application to declare the award unperformable.
3. Pearl entered a statutory judgment in terms of the award (31 October
   2007). D1-D2 applied to set that judgment aside; only then did they
   disclose the divestment.
4. Pearl commenced a common law action to enforce the award. A first-instance
   challenge to that action succeeded, but the Court of Appeal allowed
   Pearl's appeal.
5. Quantum was assessed over an 8-day hearing plus two further rulings and an
   appeal. At the leave stage, D1-D2 now seek to run an entirely new
   counterfactual on remitter, unsupported by pleading or evidence below.

## Instructions

Pearl asks: (a) may the common law action be brought as an alternative where
the statutory judgment already exists, and when must an election occur;
(b) what is the cause of action and the correct approach to damages; (c) can
D1-D2 re-run quantum with a new counterfactual on remitter?
