# Chronology — extract

- **1983.** Madam Tang Yin holds the Government lease of land near Nam Wai, New Territories. She grants a sub-lease to Mr Cheong Lam To (unregistered under the LRO).
- **25 January 1986.** Madam Tang executes a Deed of Gift purporting to assign the residue of the Government lease to her adopted son Mr To Shui Ki and herself as joint tenants.
- **1990.** Madam Tang dies.
- **1997.** Mr To Shui Ki, claiming by survivorship, sells to a property company; the company sub-divides and sells plots to individual purchasers (the plaintiffs among them).
- **Later.** Mr Cheong Lam To or his licencees remain in possession. Two plot purchasers sue for possession.
- **First instance (Alexander Stock SC, DHCJ).** Three Ord 14A questions answered against the appellant's estate; plaintiffs win.
- **Court of Appeal (Barma, G Lam, Chow JJA).** Appeal dismissed.
- **17 April 2026.** Court of Final Appeal judgment (FACV 1/2026).
